Clotr for teams

Clotr is free at work too. An office can roll it out to every computer through browser policy, and everyone gets the same free Clotr. It reports nothing to the organization.

What an organization never sees

What your people type, what Clotr caught, or when. Clotr sends nothing to anyone, so there's nothing to collect and no admin dashboard.

Roll it out

Ready-made presets for developers, for offices that handle client names, and for clinics, plus a printable page that shows the policy is applied. A team can add its own kinds of detail, like a matter number, and when its policy asks before sending, attached files wait for an answer too.

Roll it out to a team

Why teams matter to Clotr

I'm building Clotr toward a privacy engine people can rely on everywhere they type, and teams are what drive that work. When an office needs Clotr to catch something new, it lands in the engine everyone gets for free. Clotr is funded by me personally, and anything teams contribute goes into development. Teamwork lifts us all.

Help putting it in place

I'm putting together a pack for teams that want help: an AI use policy you can edit, a sheet that answers the AI questions on cyber-insurance renewals, and install guides. It isn't for sale yet. If it would help your team, email me and tell me what you'd need.
