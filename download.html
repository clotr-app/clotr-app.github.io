Or install it by hand

On Firefox, click Add to Firefox above: that's the newest version, straight from the store.

On Chrome, Brave or Edge, and want the newest version before the store catches up?

  1. Download the latest clotr-<version>.zip from Releases and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (brave://extensions or edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder you unzipped.
