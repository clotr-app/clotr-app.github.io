Available

The browser extension

Free, from your browser's own store. Chrome, Brave, Edge and Firefox on a computer.

Brave uses the Chrome Web Store.

Then pin Clotr to your toolbar. A welcome page opens with a practice box, and asks which email and chat apps you use.

Or install it by hand

On Firefox, or want the newest version first?

  1. Download the latest clotr-<version>.zip from Releases and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder you unzipped.
